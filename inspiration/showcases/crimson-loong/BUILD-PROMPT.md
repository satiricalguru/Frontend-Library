# Build prompt: a Crimson Loong–class real-time film

This is a complete brief for an AI coding agent (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Copilot, Gemini CLI…) to build a
90-second, zero-asset, single-HTML dragon film of the same scope, structure and look as
[赤龍 · Crimson Loong](https://chetaslua.github.io/crimson-loong/) by ChetasLua. Background and stills:
[README.md](README.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Original code only.** The Crimson Loong repository declares no licence, so its source, stills and score are all
> rights reserved. Use the live film and its stills as a visual reference, and these notes as a specification; write
> every line yourself. three.js is MIT: keep its licence banner in the built page.

## How to use it

1. Start an empty repository and paste **the prompt** below as your first message.
2. Let the agent work milestone by milestone (section 12). Don't skip the verification step at the end of each one:
   that's where the quality comes from.
3. Keep the beat sheet and world anchors frozen once the camera and choreography are set; retune values, never rename.

## The prompt

**Copy everything from here to the end of the file.**

You are a senior graphics engineer and technical director. Build **"Crimson Loong"-class real-time film**: a looping
90-second animated film that plays live in the browser from **one self-contained HTML file**, using **three.js r180
only**. Every mesh, texture, shader, sound and note is generated by code at load. Work in milestones, verify each one
against the acceptance shots, and keep the code organised exactly as specified.

### 1. Story

At dusk, a **red loong** (Chinese dragon, crimson body, gold mane) lies coiled on a karst limestone spire above a
valley of red maples and vermilion temple halls, with a jade lake and waterfalls below. It wakes, crests the spire
and roars fire at the sky. Thunder answers; a pale rift opens in a storm-cloud tower; a **white loong**
(pearlescent, silver mane, icy blue accents) pours out and spirals down. They circle the spire eye to eye, roar,
collide twice in mid-air, chase with fire and a lightning bolt that shatters rock, lock and entwine, and plunge into
the lake in a huge splash. They tear apart, meet head on with a fire jet against a frost-lightning jet, and the
meeting point detonates in a white-out. A glowing **pearl** is left behind; both loongs spiral upward after it and
vanish into the clouds. The film fades and loops.

### 2. Hard constraints

- **One HTML file.** three.js r180 inlined, plus your code. No other library: no three.js add-ons, no
  post-processing package, no physics engine, no audio library.
- **Zero assets.** No image, model, font, HDRI or audio file anywhere; no `data:` URIs; no loaders. Generate
  everything procedurally at load (geometry, textures via GPU bakes or canvas drawing, audio via synthesis).
- **Zero network** after the page opens. It must run from `file://` as well as from a server.
- **WebGL 2** with half-float render targets. If unavailable, show one plain line under the title, never a blank frame.
- **Deterministic:** everything on screen and in the score is a pure function of film time `t` (0–90 s, looping).
  Any frame can be rendered alone, in any order. State that needs memory (lake rings, torn leaves, swinging lanterns)
  reads tables precomputed at load.
- **60 fps** at up to ~3.7 megapixels on an Apple-silicon laptop GPU, with adaptive quality (section 10).
- **Controls:** click, tap or `M` starts sound (and later mutes/unmutes); `Space` pauses picture and sound together
  and resumes from the same film time; pointer movement nudges the camera slightly. No other UI.
- Keep the three.js MIT copyright banner in the built page and its licence text in `vendor/LICENSE`.

Modules share one global namespace `G` (renderer, scene, camera, uniforms, a `G.tick` list of `update(t)` functions,
registered in build order; the water mirror registers last). Each module exposes `build(scene, …)` (async allowed,
reports loader progress) and pure samplers where others need them.

### 4. Look and palette

- **Light:** late golden hour turning to dusk over the 90 s. Low warm sun, cool blue sky fill from above, warm bounce
  from rock and leaves below. Red lives in the low sky, blue above it. During the storm a cold rim light comes from the
  rift (no shadows), flashing with lightning.
- **Valley:** red and orange maples, dark green Huangshan pines on ledges, grey-gold jointed limestone, vermilion
  timber halls with dark tiled roofs and upturned eaves, gilt edge lines, lit paper windows, red lanterns. Jade-teal lake.
  Mist sea between spires; cumulus towers; one bruised storm tower.
- **Red loong:** deep crimson plate scales with darker edges and warm sheen, gold-ochre mane, belly plates paler,
  antlers dark gold, eyes amber with a hot glow when charging. **White loong:** pearl-white scales with faint
  blue-violet iridescence, silver mane, icy eyes.
- **Shell/loader:** background `#0a0506`, radial ember glow `#2a0b0c` behind the title; title glyph 赤龍 in `#d8322a`
  with a soft red glow; small caps text `#e9c9a4`; progress bar accent `#e9a15c`; serif CJK stack
  `"Songti SC","Noto Serif SC",Georgia,serif` (system fonts only, nothing downloaded). Bottom-right pill "click for
  sound"; bottom-left credit line. Both fade in after load.

### 5. The frozen beat sheet

80 bpm: 0.75 s per beat, 3 s per bar, 30 bars. Store as `Film.T` (seconds). Picture, effects and score all key off
these values and nothing else.

| Key | t (s) | Event |
|:--|--:|:--|
| (open) | 0 | Camera rises from 1 m above the lake bank, behind grass and a yellow shrub, out to the right over the lake; foreground, bank, pavilions, halls and spires parallax against each other |
| EMERGE | 9.0 | Red slides round the front of the spire |
| DRUMS | 15.0 | Drums enter; red climbs and crests the spire |
| CHARGE | 21.4 | Red inhales; throat glow builds |
| ROAR / ROAR_END | 24.0 / 27.0 | Red roars fire at the sky |
| OMEN | 27.0 | Thunder; lightning inside the cloud tower; pale rift opens |
| DESCENT | 30.0 | White breaks out of the cloud and spirals down |
| FACEOFF | 36.0 | They circle the spire, eye to eye |
| WROAR / WROAR_END | 39.0 / 41.2 | White's frost-lightning roar; red snarls back |
| CLASH1 | 42.0 | First mid-air collision |
| FIRE2 | 46.5 | Chase: red breathes fire across white's path (~1.6 s) |
| BOLT | 49.5 | White's lightning bolt misses red and shatters rock |
| CLASH2 | 51.0 | Second collision: bite, lock, entwine |
| SPLASH | 57.0 | Entwined tumble hits the lake; they tear apart |
| BEAM | 63.0 | Fire jet vs frost-lightning jet, head on |
| BLAST | 67.5 | The meeting point detonates (white-out envelope) |
| FINALE | 69.0 | Both spiral upward after the pearl |
| GONG | 84.0 | They vanish into the clouds |
| OUT | 86.6 | Fade out; loop at 90 |

`Film.A` holds named world anchors in metres (rift, clash1, boltHit, clash2, splash, exitR, exitW, plus beamMid and
pearl, rewritten each frame from pure samplers `Film.beamMidAt(t)`, `Film.pearlAt(t)`). `Film.STRIKES` lists the
OMEN lightning times; `Film.flashAt(t)` is the lightning envelope (each hit a fast flash with a re-strike 0.11 s
later; the frame before a flash is slightly darker). `Film.post` carries `{ fade, exposure, shake, flash, god, bloom,
white }` for the post pipeline.

### 6. World layout (scale in metres)

A valley corridor running across the frame: a **gate cliff** at the back with the temple gate and halls on terraces
below it; the **main spire** (where red lies coiled, rail "seated" on the rock) slightly off centre; secondary spires
and cliff walls framing left and right; a **lake** in front of the gate with waterfalls falling from the gate cliff;
pavilions and a yellow shrub on the near bank. The camera stays in the corridor in front of the gate, over the lake.
Loong bodies are **150–172 m** long and move at up to **75 m/s**; size everything so a loong reads as huge against the
halls.

### 7. Systems specification

**7.1 Time, film and camera (`88_film.js`)**
- The camera is a crane: position keys `[t, x, y, z, fov]` interpolated with a **monotone cubic** (never stops dead
  between marks). It looks at a weighted blend of targets keyed against the beats: red head, white head, rift, lake,
  where the jets meet, the pearl, where the bolt lands. Head targets are smoothed over ~1 s (longer while white
  spirals down) so the frame leads and trails instead of twitching.
- Keyed framing offsets (yaw/pitch in degrees) put subjects on thirds and leave room for a breath to cross the frame.
- Add a gentle handheld breath (low-frequency noise), shake from `Film.post.shake`, and a small pointer nudge.
- Keep a 16:9 vertical field of view; taller windows keep the horizontal field instead (Vert+).
- `Film.apply(t, camera)` also drives the sky's storm controls, the rift light and grade pulses.

**7.2 Daylight and shared GLSL (`05_glsl.js`)**
- One "daylight" uniform set every material reads, patched into built-in materials with
  `Material.prototype.onBeforeCompile`: cloud-shadow drift, the storm's shadow, the dusk line, the sun's tint going from
  gold to ember. Provide a CPU copy (`Daylight.sunAt(x, y, z, t)`) for anything placed on the CPU.
- Height fog and aerial haze toward the dusk sky colour; a shared wind function (gusts plus the wake field).

**7.3 Sky (`20_sky.js`)**
- An analytic dusk gradient (red low, blue high, sun glow).
- **Ray-march cumulus once at load** into four fisheye layers: torn tufts low over the valley, the storm tower (in a
  window of its own), near towers, far towers with small cumulus. Each drifts with the valley wind at its own rate so
  layers slide past each other. At display time each layer flows along a baked velocity field (two copies swapped in
  turn so nothing runs away); the sky swells and sinks on slow standing waves; turrets climb behind the storm tower.
  Composite front to back over streaming cirrus. Storm controls: darkening, internal lightning, the opening rift, rain.

**7.4 Rock (`30_rock.js`)**
- Spires and cliffs from **displaced parametric surfaces**. Model jointed limestone as a block field: vertical joints
  cut columns, bedding planes cut columns into blocks, each block slightly proud or recessed with its own tilt.
- Implement the block field **twice with the same integer hash**: on the CPU to displace the mesh (silhouette,
  shadows, AO) and in the fragment shader, evaluated per pixel at the vertex's undisplaced position (a custom
  attribute), so every drawn joint lands exactly on a geometric step and facets stay crisp at any distance.
- Add bedding, fractures, lichen and wetness from baked detail maps. Record planting sites (ledges, terraces) for
  flora and buildings.

**7.5 Architecture (`35_arch.js`)**
- Timber-frame temple halls three hundred years weathered: columns, brackets, beams, tiled roofs with curved ridges
  and upturned eaves, gilt edge lines, lit paper windows, a gate. Write every part into one indexed buffer per material
  (timber/paint/metal, stone, roof, paper, cord) in world space, with per-vertex paint colour, baked occlusion, height
  above the foot (for weathering), member width (edge wear) and a seed.
- Lanterns are pendulums driven by the wind and wake field (pure in `t` via precomputed responses); cloth banners sway.

**7.6 Flora (`40_flora.js`)**
- Instanced maples, pines, plum blossom, shrubs, ferns, grass, spider lilies, leaf litter and cliff plants at the
  recorded sites, with a frozen random stream so placement never changes.
- Leaves are small sprays on a canvas-drawn data atlas (shade, per-leaf hue shift, twig, coverage), coloured per tree
  from a palette, lit on the near face and **through the leaf** from the far face (backlit leaves glow), shaded inside
  the crown, receiving the sun's shadow map. Sway with wind; answer the wake field.
- Instances are stored in random order so a low tier can draw the first half for an even thinning.

**7.7 The loongs: look (`58_dlook.js`)**
- Palettes as data (`{ red, white }`) reaching the GPU as uniforms or vertex colours, so both dragons share compiled
  programs.
- Body: a tube along the spine with a radius profile (neck, chest swell, tapering tail, belly flattening). A
  **plate-scale shader** in body coordinates (along/around): overlapping scales from a cellular pattern, darker
  edges, sheen and iridescence, paler belly plates, wetness after the lake.
- Head, teeth, antlers, lids and frills **sculpted with SDFs** (`10_sdf.js`: tapered capsules, bezier spikes,
  smooth unions, pressed-on raised parts) and meshed once with **surface nets**, shared between palettes.
  Animate jaw, lids and nostrils; eyes glow when charging.
- Mane as tufts/cards along the neck and spine, whiskers as tubes with secondary motion, four legs with claws.

**7.8 The loongs: motion (`60_dragon.js`)**
- Each body glides along a **rail**: a path keyed in film time built from `Film.A` anchors. Sample the spine by
  arclength; frame it with position, tangent, normal, binormal.
- Layer the acting, every layer a pure function of `t`: (1) travel along the rail at a keyed speed; (2) a body
  undulation wave travelling tailward, scaled by speed; (3) head aim at its target with neck lag; (4) poses for
  charge, roar, snarl, bite, entwine and recoil keyed to the beats.
- Red's coil sits on the spire (`seatAt(s)` tells how firmly the rail lies on rock); the entwined tumble at CLASH2 →
  SPLASH is one shared choreography.
- Publish state per dragon each frame: `{ head, fwd, up, mouth, neck, roar, charge, jaw, breath, glow, speed, s, vis }`.

**7.9 Wake (`66_wake.js`)**
- A live wind field as uniforms: 8 points down each body (position, radius, velocity) plus the pressure fronts of
  the roars, clashes, bolt, splash and blast, each a shell travelling outward at finite speed from its start time.
- `vec3 clWake(vec3 p)` returns the added wind: air shoved ahead of the head, dragged beside the body, sucked in behind
  the tail, and the fronts. Mirror it in JS (`Wake.windAt(p, t)`). Plants, dust, stones, mist, lanterns and water read it.

**7.10 Water (`68_water.js`)**
- A lake with a **true planar mirror**: render the scene from the camera reflected in the water plane (clip plane,
  reduced resolution, after this frame's lights and leaves), distort it with wind normals and ripples.
- Depth and shore from a baked bed map (absorption colour by depth, shore foam). Ring ripples from uniforms (centre,
  front radius, amplitude), a current, wakes where bodies cross the surface (bow sheets), and a big crown/column
  **splash** at SPLASH with spray falling back.
- Waterfalls as sheets draped on the gate cliff with scrolling streaks, foam at the foot and rolling spray.

**7.11 Effects (`70_fx.js`)**
- **Fire breath:** a hot core ribbon plus billowing particles along the mouth direction, heat glow and embers; lights
  the scene briefly.
- **Frost-lightning breath:** a pale blue jet with branching arcs and ice crystals.
- **Lightning:** branching bolts inside and out of the storm tower at `Film.STRIKES`, with the re-strike envelope.
- **Clashes:** shock rings in the air, debris, mist blown outward (via wake fronts).
- **Bolt:** hits rock at `Film.A.boltHit`, shatters it into falling chunks and dust.
- **Beam ball:** where the jets meet it grows, wobbles and is pushed back and forth, then **blasts** with a white-out
  (`Film.post.white`) and a pressure front.
- **Pearl:** a glowing sphere born at the blast point, drifting up in front of the spire, then climbing a widening
  spiral round the spire to the rift; both loongs chase it.
- Aftermath: drifting embers, falling leaves, dimmed lanterns. Allocate everything once in `build()`.

**7.12 Air (`72_air.js`)**
- Mist that travels: a sea of cloud between the spires, banner clouds off the summits, tatters across the valley,
  spray off the falls, thin wisps on the lake. Puffs move along baked stream lines on the wind clock, flow round the
  spires, part around and are dragged by the bodies, and are thrown outward by each front. Erosion noise lives in
  stream space so a stream reads as one torn body. Cloud "falls" pour over the gate cliff lip.
- Birds (cranes) crossing early, drifting leaves, smoke from the halls, fireflies as dusk deepens, gust fronts that
  sweep visibly through grass and trees.

**7.13 Post pipeline (`80_post.js`)**
- MSAA HDR (half float) scene → screen-space ambient occlusion → a soft FX layer (mist and sprites depth-tested by hand
  against the scene depth) → **bloom in 4 steps** (first downsample firefly-weighted, later steps a wide 13-tap kernel)
  → **sun shafts** marched at quarter resolution through the haze, querying the sun's shadow map and the cloud-shadow
  field so beams stand between shadows and move with the clouds → grade.
- Grade: exposure from `Film.post`, filmic tonemap, warm/cool split toning, flash and white-out envelopes, vignette,
  fine grain, slight lens chromatic fringe. Camera shake applied here too.

**7.14 Score and sound (`85_audio.js`)**
- Synthesise **everything** in a Web Worker at load (`synth()` self-contained), returned as audio buffers and played
  through the Web Audio API with a synthesised reverb impulse. Sound stays off until the first click/tap/M.
- Red loong's music: erhu (bowed string, vibrato, bow noise), dizi (breathy flute with membrane buzz), guzheng
  (plucked, Karplus-Strong style), taiko and gong (inharmonic partials) in **G minor pentatonic**.
- White loong's music: bowed glass, xiao and bells on a four-note motif **B–F♯–E–D** (the bright side of G).
- Foley: thunder (filtered noise bursts + sub rumble) on the strikes, two dragon voices (formant roars and snarls),
  fire whoosh, frost crackle, clash impacts, rock shatter, the splash, the blast, the closing gong at GONG.
- Every hit is placed from the same beat sheet on the 80 bpm grid. Once sound plays, the **film follows the audio
  clock** (only while that clock is seen to run; otherwise fall back to the film's own clock without a jump). Space
  stops both and resumes both from the same film time.

**7.15 Boot, loop and quality (`90_main.js`)**
- Ask for half-float targets before building anything. Build in stages behind the loader with progress words
  ("waking", "carving stone", "growing maples", …) and the progress bar; the loader fades out over 1.2 s.
- Lights: one sun with a shadow map, a sky/bounce fill that deepens as the take runs, and the cold rift light (no
  shadow) at intensity 0 until `Film.apply` drives it.
- Frame pacing: draw at 60 fps; on 120 Hz displays draw every second refresh. **Adaptive quality** levels 0–5 scale the
  pixel count from 1.0 to 0.5; below 0.7 also halve the shadow map, MSAA and plant instance counts; the last level
  holds 0.5 and draws every second frame (a steady 30). A level that holds its rate for 4 s climbs back one step; a
  failed climb doubles the wait. Use GPU timer queries where available.
- Resize: rebuild targets at most once per 90 ms. Lost WebGL context: hold the film, say so, rebuild at the same film
  time when the context returns.

- `tools/perf.py`: average and worst frame cost for each beat-sheet section.
- `tools/imgdiff.py`: compare a capture with a reference still and report the difference.

### 10. Definition of done

- One `index.html`, under ~3 MB, opens from `file://` with no network requests (check DevTools → Network).
- Plays the full 90 s story with all beats, loops seamlessly, and any `?t=` frame renders identically on repeat.
- Holds 60 fps on a recent laptop GPU at default quality; degrades smoothly and recovers.
- Sound starts on the first click, stays in sync through pause and resume, and every hit lands on its beat.
- All seven acceptance shots read clearly; no blank frames, no NaN flashes, no popping between quality levels.
- The page credits three.js (MIT banner kept) and contains no copied code from other projects.

### 11. Working method

- Prefer a few large merged buffers and instanced meshes over many objects; keep draw calls low.
- Bake anything expensive at load (clouds, detail maps, response tables) and keep display time to compositing.
- Keep every effect a pure function of `t`; never accumulate state across frames.
- Write the same function on CPU and GPU when geometry and shading must agree.
- Use `onBeforeCompile` patches so built-in materials share fog, haze, cloud shadows and wind.
- Commit after each milestone with a capture of the acceptance shots that milestone affects.
